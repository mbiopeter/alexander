ALEXENDER — A SMALL PROJECT BRIEF

A few notes are enough to start. Fill in what you know and leave the rest open.

1. THE GOAL
What should the website, app, script or fix help you do?
Who will use it?

2. THE STARTING POINT
Is this a new idea or an existing project?
Which tools or technologies are already involved?
Is there a public link or an example you can share?

3. THE ESSENTIALS
Which three things must the first version do?
What can wait until later?

4. THE RESULT
How will we know the work is useful and complete?
For a bug: what did you expect, what happened, and how can it be reproduced?

5. PRACTICAL DETAILS (OPTIONAL)
Project or business name:
Your preferred way to communicate:
Target date, or flexible:
Budget range, or let's discuss:

6. HANDOVER
Who will use or maintain the finished work?
What instructions or training would help?
